How the code UBIN0550841 is built

UBIN is the bank part: the letters of Union Bank of India. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 550841 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 641026008 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 641 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 641012), 026 is the bank and 008 is the branch.
